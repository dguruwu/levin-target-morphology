# START HERE — what this repository is

A third-party research collection (not affiliated with Prof. Michael Levin) collecting
**fetched primary sources** and **clearly-labeled speculative constructions** relevant to
one open question in his own words (Bessonov & Levin 2015):

> "what mechanisms implement recognition of correct morphology that signals a stop condition?"

## The published results we build on (abstracts fetched, quotes verbatim)

- **Target morphology and cell memory** (Bessonov, Levin et al. 2015, PMID 26889161):
  cells keep a *pattern memory*; damage changes the signal distribution and the difference
  from the stored pattern drives correction — an error-minimization using memory.
- **A linear-encoding model of target morphology** (Lobo et al. 2014, PMID 24402915):
  regenerators (deer antlers, planaria, fiddler crabs) work toward *pre-specified goal
  states*, alterable "specifically and stably by injuries."
- **Knowing one's place** (Friston & Levin 2015, PMID 25788538): cell groups *model* shape.
- **Long-term stochastic editing of regenerative anatomy via bioelectric gradients**
  (Durant et al. 2017, PMID 28538159): the pattern is "permanently rewritten" by a brief
  bioelectric perturbation; two-headed regenerates; a **"multistable, epigenetic anatomical
  switch"**; and the **cryptic phenotype** — the altered target stored invisibly in a
  morphologically normal worm until it is cut.
- **The scaling of goals via homeostasis** (arXiv 2211.08522): evolution scales homeostatic
  setpoints into goal states.

## Our speculative constructions (tagged [EXTENSION] wherever they appear)

1. **A Krein-space reading of the multistable switch**: several stable states cohabiting one
   animal = mixed signatures in an indefinite metric; the cryptic state = an *unchosen
   direction* of the metric, present while unexpressed.
2. **The stop condition as an act, not a chemistry**: recognition of "correct" is the
   operation that issues the metric — testable prediction: the stop lives in the bioelectric
   read/write act.
3. **Sleep-rewritability**: if the target is read/written through the bioelectric channel,
   modulating that channel during the offline/sleep-like state should have disproportionate
   effect on the stored target. Experiment design in issue #2.
4. **Memory = negentropy** (I = N): stored counterfactual as accumulated negentropy.
