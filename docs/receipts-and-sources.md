# Receipts and sources (fetched abstracts)

**Target morphology and cell memory: a model of regenerative pattern formation.**
Bessonov N, Levin M, Morozova N, Reinberg N, Tosenberger A, Volpert V.
*Neural Regeneration Research* 2015;10(12):1901-5. PMID [26889161](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26889161/).
> "...all cells communicate with each other and keep the value of the total signal
> received... an error minimization process that uses signaling memory to achieve pattern
> correction." Named open question: "what mechanisms implement recognition of correct
> morphology that signals a stop condition."

**A linear-encoding model explains the variability of the target morphology in
regeneration.** Lobo D, Solano M, Bubenik GA, Levin M. *J R Soc Interface* 2014. PMID [24402915](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24402915/).
> "their target morphology can be altered specifically and stably by injuries in
> particular locations" (deer antlers, planarian worms, fiddler crabs).

**Knowing one's place: a free-energy approach to pattern regulation.** Friston K, Levin M,
Sengupta B, Pezzulo G. *J R Soc Interface* 2015. PMID [25788538](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25788538/).
> "morphogenesis could be understood in terms of cellular information processing and the
> ability of cell groups to model shape."

**Long-Term, Stochastic Editing of Regenerative Anatomy via Targeting Endogenous
Bioelectric Gradients.** Durant F, Morokuma J, Fields C, Williams K, Adams DS, Levin M.
*Biophys J* 2017;112(10):2231-2243. PMID [28538159](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28538159/).
> "regenerating planarians' normal anterior-posterior pattern can be permanently rewritten
> by a brief perturbation of endogenous bioelectrical networks... a multistable, epigenetic
> anatomical switch... the altered regenerative bodyplan is stored in seemingly normal
> planaria via global patterns of cellular resting potential."

**The scaling of goals via homeostasis: an evolutionary simulation, experiment and
analysis.** Pio-Lopez L, Bischof J, LaPalme JV, Levin M. [arXiv:2211.08522](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.08522) (2022).
> "evolution pivoted the collective intelligence of cells during morphogenesis of the
> body into traditional behavioral intelligence by scaling up the goal states at the
> center of homeostatic processes."
